How to practise
  1. Press ▶ Listen (or Space). The beat loops.
  2. Listen to it once all the way round.
  3. Then play along. Try to land it once, then twice, then 4 or 5 loops in a row.
  • Too fast? Press − to slow it down, then + to work back up (5 BPM a step, works while it plays; ↓ / ↑ keys too). Full speed is about 184 BPM.
  • ⚙ has the rest: count-in, click, volume.
  • The orange boxes in the count grid are the push: the kick comes early, on the "&".
  • Headphones (Bluetooth too) are fine; there's nothing to plug in.
  • No sound on an iPhone or iPad? Turn off silent mode (the side switch) and turn the volume up.
  • ⛶ full screen (top right) hides everything else. Esc comes back.
